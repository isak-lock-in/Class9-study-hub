🟢 Question 1 — Easy

State whether the following number is rational or irrational:

√2


Retries available: 2

Can √2 be written in the form p/q where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0?

Step 1: A rational number can be written as p/q.

Step 2: √2 cannot be expressed in the form p/q using integers p and q.

Step 3: Therefore √2 is irrational.

Answer: Irrational

🟢 Question 2 — Easy → Medium

Determine whether the decimal expansion of 7/20 is terminating or non-terminating recurring.


Retries available: 2

Factor the denominator. A rational number has a terminating decimal when its denominator in lowest form has only 2 and/or 5 as prime factors.

Step 1: Look at the denominator.

20 = 2 × 2 × 5

Step 2: The denominator contains only the prime factors 2 and 5.

Step 3: Therefore the decimal expansion terminates.

In fact:

7/20 = 0.35

Answer: Terminating

🟡 Question 3 — Medium

Simplify the following surd:

√72


Retries available: 2

Find the largest perfect-square factor of 72.

Step 1: Write 72 as a product containing a perfect square.

72 = 36 × 2

Step 2: Take the square root of each factor.

√72 = √36 × √2

Step 3: √36 = 6

Therefore:

√72 = 6√2

Answer: 6√2

🟠 Question 4 — Hard

Simplify:

√50 + √8


Retries available: 2

Simplify each surd separately before adding them.

Step 1: Simplify √50.

√50 = √(25 × 2)
= 5√2

Step 2: Simplify √8.

√8 = √(4 × 2)
= 2√2

Step 3: Add the like surds.

5√2 + 2√2 = 7√2

Answer: 7√2

🔴 Question 5 — Hardest

Rationalize the denominator:

1/√3


Retries available: 2

Multiply both numerator and denominator by √3.

Step 1: Start with:

1/√3

Step 2: Multiply the numerator and denominator by √3.

(1/√3) × (√3/√3)

Step 3: Simplify the denominator.

√3 × √3 = 3

Therefore:

√3/3

Answer: √3/3
