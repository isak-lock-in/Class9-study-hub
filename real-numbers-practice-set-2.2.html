🟢 Question 1 — Easy

Which of the following is a rational number?

√5, 3/7, √11, π


Retries available: 2

A rational number can be expressed in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

Step 1: Check each number.

√5, √11 and π are irrational.

3/7 is already written as a ratio of two integers.

Answer: 3/7

🟢 Question 2 — Easy → Medium

Determine whether the decimal expansion of 13/40 is terminating or non-terminating recurring.


Retries available: 2

Factor 40 into primes and check whether only 2 and 5 occur.

Step 1: Factor the denominator.

40 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 5

Step 2: The denominator contains only 2 and 5.

Therefore the decimal expansion terminates.

Step 3: 13/40 = 0.325

Answer: Terminating

🟡 Question 3 — Medium

Simplify:

√98


Retries available: 2

Write 98 as a perfect square multiplied by another number.

Step 1: 98 = 49 × 2

Step 2: √98 = √(49 × 2)

= √49 × √2

Step 3: √49 = 7

Therefore:

√98 = 7√2

Answer: 7√2

🟠 Question 4 — Hard

Simplify:

√75 − √12


Retries available: 2

Simplify both square roots before subtracting.

Step 1: Simplify √75.

√75 = √(25 × 3)
= 5√3

Step 2: Simplify √12.

√12 = √(4 × 3)
= 2√3

Step 3: Subtract the like surds.

5√3 − 2√3 = 3√3

Answer: 3√3
