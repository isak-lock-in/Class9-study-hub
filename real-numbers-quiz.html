1. Rational or Irrational

Which of the following numbers is irrational?

💡 Explanation:

√2 cannot be expressed as a ratio of two integers.

Therefore: √2 is irrational.

2. Decimal Expansion

What type of decimal expansion does 7/20 have?

💡 Explanation:

20 = 2² × 5

The denominator contains only the prime factors 2 and 5.

Therefore the decimal expansion terminates.

7/20 = 0.35

Answer: Terminating

3. Simplifying Surds

Simplify:

√48 + √27

💡 Explanation:

√48 = √(16 × 3)
= 4√3

√27 = √(9 × 3)
= 3√3

Therefore:
4√3 + 3√3 = 7√3

4. Rationalisation

Which is the rationalised form of:

5/√3

💡 Explanation:

Start with:
5/√3

Multiply numerator and denominator by √3:

(5/√3) × (√3/√3)

= 5√3/3

Answer: 5√3/3

5. Difference of Squares

Simplify:

(√5 + √2)(√5 − √2)

💡 Explanation:

Use:

(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

Therefore:
(√5)² − (√2)²

= 5 − 2

= 3

6. Euclid's Division Algorithm

What is the HCF of 36 and 48?

💡 Explanation:

Using Euclid's algorithm:

48 = 36 × 1 + 12
36 = 12 × 3 + 0

Therefore: HCF = 12

7. Product of HCF and LCM

If the HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 180, what is the product of the two numbers?

💡 Explanation:

Product of two numbers:

HCF × LCM

= 6 × 180
= 1080
