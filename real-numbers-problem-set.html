🟢 Problem 1 — Easy

1. Rational or Irrational

Determine whether the following number is rational or irrational:

√50


💡 Step-by-step solution:

√50

= √(25 × 2)

= 5√2

Since √2 is irrational, 5√2 is also irrational.

Therefore: √50 is irrational.
🟢 Problem 2 — Easy/Medium

2. Decimal Expansion

Determine whether the decimal expansion of 13/40 is terminating or non-terminating recurring.


💡 Step-by-step solution:

The denominator is:
40 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 5

It contains only the prime factors 2 and 5.

Therefore, the decimal expansion terminates.

Check:
13/40 = 0.325

Therefore: Terminating
🟡 Problem 3 — Medium

3. Simplifying Surds

Simplify:

√75 − √12 + √27


💡 Step-by-step solution:

√75 = √(25 × 3) = 5√3

√12 = √(4 × 3) = 2√3

√27 = √(9 × 3) = 3√3

Therefore:
5√3 − 2√3 + 3√3

= (5 − 2 + 3)√3

= 6√3
🟠 Problem 4 — Hard

4. Rationalisation

Rationalise the denominator:

6/√5


💡 Step-by-step solution:

Start with:
6/√5

Multiply numerator and denominator by √5:

(6/√5) × (√5/√5)

= 6√5 / 5

Therefore: 6√5/5
🔴 Problem 5 — Challenge

5. Difference of Squares

Simplify:

(√7 + √3)(√7 − √3)


💡 Step-by-step solution:

Use the identity:

(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

Here:
a = √7
b = √3

Therefore:
(√7)² − (√3)²

= 7 − 3

= 4
