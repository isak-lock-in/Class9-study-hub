🟢 Question 1 — Easy

State whether the following number is rational or irrational:

√49


Retries available: 2

First find √49. Can the result be written as a ratio of integers?

Step 1: √49 = 7

Step 2: 7 can be written as 7/1.

Therefore, 7 is rational.

Answer: Rational

🟢 Question 2 — Easy → Medium

Determine whether the decimal expansion of 11/30 is terminating or non-terminating recurring.


Retries available: 2

Factor 30 into primes. Does it contain a prime factor other than 2 or 5?

Step 1: 30 = 2 × 3 × 5

Step 2: The denominator contains 3 as a prime factor.

A rational number has a terminating decimal only when the denominator in lowest form has no prime factors other than 2 and 5.

Answer: Non-terminating recurring

🟡 Question 3 — Medium

Simplify:

√128


Retries available: 2

Look for the largest perfect-square factor of 128.

Step 1: 128 = 64 × 2

Step 2: √128 = √(64 × 2)

= √64 × √2

Step 3: √64 = 8

Therefore:

√128 = 8√2

Answer: 8√2

🟠 Question 4 — Hard

Simplify:

√80 + √45


Retries available: 2

Simplify both surds and then combine like surds.

Step 1: Simplify √80.

√80 = √(16 × 5)
= 4√5

Step 2: Simplify √45.

√45 = √(9 × 5)
= 3√5

Step 3: 4√5 + 3√5 = 7√5

Answer: 7√5

🔴 Question 5 — Hardest

Rationalise the denominator:

5/√2


Retries available: 2

Multiply the numerator and denominator by √2.

Step 1: 5/√2

Step 2: Multiply by √2/√2.

(5/√2) × (√2/√2)

Step 3: Numerator = 5√2

Denominator: √2 × √2 = 2

Therefore:

Answer: 5√2/2
