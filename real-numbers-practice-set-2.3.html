🟢 Question 1 — Easy

Simplify:

√18


Retries available: 2

Write 18 as 9 × 2.

Step 1: 18 = 9 × 2

Step 2: √18 = √(9 × 2)

= √9 × √2

= 3√2

Answer: 3√2

🟢 Question 2 — Easy → Medium

Simplify:

√27 + √12


Retries available: 2

Simplify both square roots first.

Step 1: √27 = √(9 × 3) = 3√3

Step 2: √12 = √(4 × 3) = 2√3

Step 3: 3√3 + 2√3 = 5√3

Answer: 5√3

🟡 Question 3 — Medium

Simplify:

√48 − √27


Retries available: 2

Express both surds using √3.

Step 1: √48 = √(16 × 3) = 4√3

Step 2: √27 = √(9 × 3) = 3√3

Step 3: 4√3 − 3√3 = √3

Answer: √3

🟠 Question 4 — Hard

Rationalise the denominator:

3/√7


Retries available: 2

Multiply numerator and denominator by √7.

Step 1: 3/√7

Step 2: Multiply by √7/√7.

(3/√7) × (√7/√7)

Step 3: Numerator = 3√7

Denominator: √7 × √7 = 7

Answer: 3√7/7

🔴 Question 5 — Hardest

Simplify:

(√5 + √2)(√5 − √2)


Retries available: 2

Use (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b².

Step 1: Use the difference of squares identity.

(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

Here:
a = √5
b = √2

Step 2: (√5)² − (√2)²

= 5 − 2

= 3

Answer: 3
