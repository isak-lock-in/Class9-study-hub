🟢 Problem 1 — Easy

1. Identifying the Degree

Find the degree of:

7x4 − 3x2 + 5x − 9


💡 Step-by-step solution:

The powers of x are:
4, 2, 1 and 0.

The highest power is 4.

Therefore: Degree = 4
🟢 Problem 2 — Easy/Medium

2. Finding the Constant Term

Find the constant term of:

5x3 − 8x + 12


💡 Step-by-step solution:

The constant term is the term without a variable.

In:
5x3 − 8x + 12

the term without x is 12.

Therefore: Constant term = 12
🟡 Problem 3 — Medium

3. Number of Terms

How many terms are present in:

4x3 − 7x2 + 2x − 11


💡 Step-by-step solution:

The terms are:
4x3
−7x2
2x
−11

There are 4 terms.

Answer = 4
🟠 Problem 4 — Hard

4. Finding a Missing Coefficient

The degree of the polynomial

6x5 − 4x3 + kx2 + 9

is 5. What is the coefficient of x2 if k = −7?


💡 Step-by-step solution:

We are given:
k = −7

The x2 term is:
kx2

Therefore its coefficient is: −7
🔴 Problem 5 — Challenge

5. Constructing a Polynomial

A polynomial has degree 3, leading coefficient 5, and constant term −8.

Which expression could represent such a polynomial?


💡 Step-by-step solution:

We need:
• Degree = 3
• Leading coefficient = 5
• Constant term = −8

One valid polynomial is:
5x3 + 2x − 8

It satisfies all three conditions.
